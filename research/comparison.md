# Vietnam versus Bali — May–July 2027

> **Final numbers:** the authoritative per-head figures (Budget / Comfort plans: Vietnam ₹49,175 / ₹64,825; Bali ₹57,905 / ₹73,865 for June departures, incl. return flights — Comfort = private rooms + easy transport at the same sale fares) are in [decision-summary.md](decision-summary.md) and match the live site's calculator. Tables below are the detailed working notes (est.).


**Compiled 27 Oct 2026. Rates: 1 INR ≈ 300 VND ≈ 190 IDR; 1 USD ≈ 84 INR. All prices are INR per person (est.) unless explicitly labelled as a group/vehicle/villa total. Default group: 3.**

## Short answer

**Vietnam wins on value; Bali wins on reliably dry weather.** Choose **Vietnam, Sat 19–Mon 28 Jun 2027**, with an emphasis on Da Nang/Hoi An. Choose **Bali, Sat 12 Jun–Sun 20 Jun 2027**, if drier weather, surfing and a shared pool-villa break justify the premium. July offers Vietnam's lowest model airfare but Bali's peak-season stays. Neither destination's weather is a guarantee.

The selected plans cost **₹47,475 budget / ₹76,400 mid per head for Vietnam (est.)**, versus **₹56,550 / ₹90,800 for Bali (est.)**. The budget difference is **₹9,075 per head (est.)** even though Vietnam has one more travel day. These are funded airport-to-airport planning envelopes, not confirmed package quotes.

**Budget discipline:** Vietnam budget travellers choose beach/Marble Mountains time rather than buying every Hai Van/Ba Na upgrade. Bali budget travellers mix walking/cheap local days with shared-driver days and choose paid highlights, rather than adding every swing, surf lesson and beach-club minimum spend. Premium itinerary extras, checked bags or daily private cars must be re-quoted and added; a buffer is not an unlimited attraction allowance. Keep the requested base model separate from an upgraded itinerary quote. ## Weather, crowds and seasonality by month

Rain is a relative **0–4 planning score**, not a rain probability. Bali 0 means the driest part of the year, not “never rains.” Vietnam needs a regional description, not one whole-country promise.

| Month | Vietnam | Bali | Price/crowd implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **May** | North heating up, roughly 30–36°C; central coast sunny; south starts rainy season; model rain 2/4 | Roughly 24–31°C; dry-season transition; occasional showers; rain 1/4 | India school-holiday outbound demand; avoid Vietnam 29 Apr–3 May and price around provisional Eid al-Adha ~17 May. Bali stays are pre-peak |
| **Jun** | Hanoi/Ha Long can reach 33–38°C with humidity and storms; Da Nang/Hoi An sunny and hot; HCMC afternoon downpours; rain 3/4 | Roughly 23–30°C; generally dry; rain 0/4 | Vietnam flights soften after mid-Jun, but domestic weekends still busy. Bali shoulder pricing until late Jun; estimated school holidays start ~26 Jun |
| **Jul** | North wet/stormy with possible bay cancellations; south wet; central beaches still sunny/hot, roughly 28–37°C overall; rain 3/4 | Roughly 22–29°C; dry, breezier; rain 0/4 | Vietnam lowest airfare target. Bali peak stays roughly +30% (est.), schools plus Australian/European visitors |
| **Aug, outside window** | Northern storm risk remains; central heat; rain 4/4 in this broad model | Still dry-season peak, roughly 22–29°C; rain 0/4 | Context only: Bali stay sensitivity +35% (est.); not a recommended substitution within May–Jul |

Plan early mornings in Hanoi/HCMC and indoor/rest time during the hottest/rainiest afternoon hours. Keep Ha Long weather flexibility and do not put Penida's boat crossing immediately before a flight. Bali Arts Festival, typically mid-Jun–mid-Jul, is a plus; verify the 2027 dates. Eid and Indonesian provincial school calendars remain **verify**, not fixed bookings facts.

## What changes with 4–6 people?

Baseline **per-head costs stay unchanged**; group total simply multiplies by headcount. Flights, visas, entry tickets, food and most boat tickets do not automatically get cheaper. Shared accommodation/cars can create a **bonus**, but only if occupancy, luggage and vehicle capacity genuinely work.

| Shared-item example | 3 people | 4 people | 6 people |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Same suitably sized driver vehicle for a day | ₹1,200 pp (est.) | ₹900 pp (est.) | ₹600 pp (est.; larger vehicle may cost more) |
| Same 2-bedroom private-pool villa, legal 4-person capacity | ₹2,400 pp/night (est.) | ₹1,800 pp/night (est.) | Not a 6-person assumption |
| Same 3-bedroom villa, legal 6-person capacity | ₹3,200 pp/night (est.) | ₹2,400 pp/night (est.) | ₹1,600 pp/night (est.) |

The examples imply a whole-vehicle fee of **₹3,600/day (est.)**, a whole 2-bedroom villa of **₹7,200/night (est.)**, and a whole 3-bedroom villa of **₹9,600/night (est.)**. These are illustrative whole-booking prices, not additional per-person charges. A pool villa may still cost more than the budget stay allowance even after splitting; it is a value-for-comfort upgrade, not guaranteed savings versus a dorm. ## When Bali wins

- **Weather certainty matters most:** May–June usually has fewer wet afternoons than northern/southern Vietnam, although sea state still matters.
- **The group lives near Bangalore:** avoiding Kolkata positioning can shrink the headline Vietnam advantage. The possible nonstop also removes a connection.
- **The priority is beaches, surf, cafés and a pool villa:** 4–6 compatible sharers can buy more comfort per head; allow for lawful room occupancy and a larger driver vehicle.
- **Fewer aviation legs are preferred:** Vietnam's full north-to-south route requires two domestic flights or much longer overland travel. Bali substitutes road/boat transfers rather than eliminating travel time altogether.

Vietnam wins when the priority is the lowest funded trip cost, street food and varied cities/karst/coast, with willingness to plan around heat and rain. Allocate three to four central-coast nights instead of treating Hanoi/Ha Long as dry-season destinations.

## Combo: a different product, not a like-for-like discount

**12–29 Jun, 18 days:** 10 Vietnam days + 8 Bali days. The mandated core formula yields **₹82,400 budget / ₹1,34,400 mid per head (est.)**, including five flights, visas/levy and a combined SIM allowance; group of 3 is **₹2,47,200 / ₹4,03,200 (est.)**. With an explicit insurance/contingency reserve, fund **₹90,200 / ₹1,47,000 per head (est.)**. Full arithmetic and assumptions are in [flights](flights.md).

The **₹12,725 saving (est.)** is a **flights-only** comparison with two separate return journeys on the specified standalone dates, keeping domestic flights and 10+8 ground days equal. It is not the difference versus two complete 10+9-day trips. The combo finishes at BLR after starting at CCU, overlaps the start of Indonesian school holidays, and needs its own baggage, insurance and ground-upgrade quotes.
