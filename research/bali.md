Allow **ten calendar days away** unless a verified direct/earlier itinerary really lands on Sunday. No ninth Bali room is charged; do not promise a Monday-morning work arrival.
- **Time zones:** Bangalore is IST (UTC+5:30); Bali, Kuala Lumpur and Singapore are UTC+8, **2 hours 30 minutes ahead**. Itinerary times are Bali-local except the explicitly labelled BLR slots. All flight times are illustrative; no flight number or seasonal direct service has been confirmed.

## Why this May–July window works

- **Late May–early June:** dry-season days, generally clearer Batur mornings and more dependable Penida boat planning than the wet season. Neither clear views nor calm seas are guaranteed. Keep water activities flexible and respect harbour cancellations.
- **May is not India’s cheapest outbound month:** school-holiday demand can keep flights high. This is a Bali weather/shoulder-stay recommendation, not a claim that the quoted sale fare is bookable on these exact dates; compare later-June departures before paying.
- **July:** Bali peak season, with accommodation uplifts around **25–35% (est.)** and Australian/European summer demand. Indonesian school holidays around **26 Jun–12 Jul 2027** need official confirmation. Eid al-Adha around **17 May 2027** is also a verify-date, relevant to regional connections.
- The Bali Arts Festival, normally mid-June–mid-July, is a reason to consider a later trip; verify the 2027 programme. This chosen route is before that period. Occasional showers remain possible in the dry season; use a light rain shell, not a winter itinerary.

**Pacing decisions:**
- Day 1 is airport → connection → immigration → Ubud, with no guaranteed sunset excursion. Campuhan moves to the next morning if the flight is late.
- Day 5 is **manta snorkelling OR Diamond Beach**, never both before the return boat. The core chooses the morning snorkel, a midday harbour buffer and an afternoon sailing. For the east-coast alternative, book a driver, keep to the Diamond viewpoint, leave enough time for the long return road and re-quote the package.
- Return from Penida on 2 June, leaving **four mainland hotel nights** before the international flight. Do not book a sea crossing on the flight-home day.
- Day 9 leaves Double Six by **16:30**, allowing up to two hours on the road and three hours for international check-in. Sunset is intentionally dropped. Move all slots earlier if the actual return flight is earlier.

### Warnings

- May–June is Bali's dry-season shoulder window, not a promise of cheap Indian flights: May still overlaps school-holiday demand. July is peak with stay uplifts around 25–35% (est.); Indonesian school holidays roughly 26 Jun–12 Jul 2027 and Eid al-Adha around 17 May need official date checks.
- Scooters require the appropriate motorcycle licence plus IDP, a helmet and insurance that covers the rider and vehicle; police checks are common. The costed route uses shared cars. Do not learn to ride on Bali roads or on Nusa Penida, and respect app-driver pickup restrictions.
- Dry-season sun is strong: SPF 50, water and midday shade matter. Mount Batur is cold at 04:00 despite warm coastal weather; use a warm layer and permitted guide, and verify current volcano/trek access.
- Dry-season Penida crossings are generally more dependable, but swell can still cancel boats and snorkelling. Follow operator/harbour advice, use lifejackets and keep the four-night mainland buffer before flying. Stay off dangerous cliff descents and out of Angel's Billabong when waves can reach it.
- All fares, stays, ratings, entry rules and 2027 schedules require live checks. The nine-day itinerary ends with the Sun 20 Jun DPS night departure; a connecting flight normally lands at BLR on Mon 21 Jun, so allow a tenth home-arrival calendar day. Day 9 deliberately skips sunset to protect airport check-in.
