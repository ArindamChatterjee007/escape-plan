# When to go — best month, cheapest days, months to avoid (2027)

**Compiled 27 Oct 2026 · all fares are pattern-based estimates (est.) to confirm on Google Flights / Skyscanner · origins BLR / CCU · destinations HAN / SGN / DAD and DPS.**

> **Short version:** for the squad's May–July window, **June** is the best compromise (dry season in Bali, prime season on Vietnam's central coast, Indian school-holiday fares fading). Cheapest days to fly are **Tuesday / Wednesday** out and **Tue–Thu** back; Fri–Sun are priciest. **Avoid** December (Christmas peak, Bali rains), August (Bali high season), March for Bali (Nyepi + Lebaran), and September–November for central Vietnam (storm/flood season). The interactive version with a per-month calendar of cheapest departure days is the **"When to go"** section on the site; machine-readable data is in `docs/data/months.js`.

## A) 2027 month-by-month comparison

**Bottom line:** June is the best compromise for your **May–July preference**, particularly for Bali and central Vietnam. With complete freedom, I would favour **March for a multi-region Vietnam trip** and **early September for Bali**. **February—not July—is the strongest common starting point for finding the cheapest flights**, although Vietnam’s Tết dates must be avoided.

**Important distinction:** These are **planning estimates as of 27 October 2026**, not live quotes for 2027. Flight indices, hotel multipliers and predicted savings are **(est.)**. Holiday dates are calendar facts unless marked tentative; weather figures are historical-climate guidance, not forecasts.

### Months or periods to avoid

- **Bali in March:** Nyepi shutdown, Lebaran/mudik, then Easter-related demand.

## D) Booking rules, sale periods and the 2027 calendars

### 1. Cheapest departure/return days: use a hypothesis, not a superstition

**Start searches with Tuesday/Wednesday departures and Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday returns. Then test the surrounding dates.**

The route evidence is more useful than a generic travel hack:

| Evidence | What it says | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **KAYAK CCU–HAN** | Tuesday was cheapest, about **14% below the average day**; Sunday was most expensive in its rolling sample. [4] | Strong reason to test Tuesday departures from Kolkata |
| **KAYAK BLR–DPS** | Sunday was cheapest, about **6% below average**; Saturday about **6% above average**. [3] | Do **not** automatically discard Sunday |
| **Google, October 2026 analysis** | For **US departures**, Monday outbound with Tuesday/Wednesday return saved about **14% versus weekend patterns**. [8] | Supports date flexibility, but is **not an India-specific saving promise** |

**Friday/Sunday are useful high-demand watch-outs, not universal expensive days.** Saturday can be expensive on leisure routes too. Return-day behaviour must be checked separately from outbound behaviour.

**Mid-month versus month-start:** there is no reliable “the 15th is always cheaper” rule. June 2027 demonstrates why: the 16th is a holiday. Early December can be much better than late December, while early February is precisely the wrong Vietnam window because of Tết.

### 2. Cheapest time of day

- KAYAK’s India→Vietnam aggregate currently favours **morning departures**.
- Its India→DPS aggregate favours **night departures**. [1–2]
- Therefore, compare early/overnight flights, but do not assume every red-eye is cheaper.
- Account for airport transport and an extra hotel night if the arrival time is awkward.
- **Time of departure is different from time of booking.** There is no verified India-specific rule that buying at midnight or on Tuesday afternoon produces the lowest fare.

Google’s 2026 US analysis found only about a **1.4% booking-day difference** between its cheapest and most expensive purchase weekdays—much less important than the actual itinerary. [8]

- **Start alerts now.**

- If a genuinely attractive sale appears earlier, book it rather than waiting for an exact “sweet spot.”
- For three people, availability at the lowest fare bucket can matter more than a theoretical optimal booking week.

### 5. Fare-alert checklist

1. Compare **BLR and CCU separately** against **HAN, SGN, DAD and DPS**—eight route combinations.
2. Use Google Flights’ **date grid/price graph**, plus Skyscanner’s **whole-month view** and alerts.
3. Search **three adults**, not just one; a single cheap seat does not establish the group price.
4. Compare the **same baggage allowance and connection quality**.
5. For Vietnam, compare **open-jaw itineraries** against the cost of backtracking.
6. Verify fares on the airline website before paying.
7. Do not treat a self-transfer or separate positioning ticket as equivalent to a protected connection.
8. Save a refundable hotel option while tracking flights, particularly for Bali’s June/July school-holiday period.

- **Nyepi:** **Monday, 8 March 2027**. Plan for the customary approximately **06:00 on 8 March–06:00 on 9 March** shutdown **(operational timing est.; reconfirm the airport notice)**. Visitors normally remain within their accommodation; this is not merely a day when some shops close. [23,25]
