## ✅ Recommendation: Vietnam · Sat 19 Jun → Mon 28 Jun 2027 (10 days) · from Kolkata

| | Budget (dorms, street food, buses) | Mid-range (private rooms, more tours) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Return flights CCU→HAN / SGN→CCU (IndiGo nonstop, June fare ×1.05) | ₹15,225 | ₹22,050 |
| 10 days on the ground (stay + food + local transport + activities) | ₹23,000 (₹2,300/day) | ₹41,000 (₹4,100/day) |
| Extras: e-visa ₹2,100 · 2 domestic flights ₹4,100/₹6,800 · SIM ₹350 · insurance ₹900 · cruise/Hải Vân top-up ₹1,700 · 8 % buffer | ₹10,950 | ₹13,350 |
| **Per head, all-in** | **₹49,175** | **₹76,400** |
| **Group of 3** | **₹1,47,525** | **₹2,29,200** |

**Start:** Sat 19 Jun — be at Kolkata (CCU) by ~03:30, IndiGo CCU→HAN ~06:30, land Hanoi ~11:00 (est.).
**Return:** Mon 28 Jun — IndiGo SGN→CCU ~20:30, land Kolkata ~22:30 the same night (est.). *Flying from Bangalore instead adds ≈ ₹1,600 per head (BLR→SGN/HAN nonstops ₹16,000–16,500).*

**Day by day (ground plan):**
1. Kolkata → Hanoi · land, cool off, Old Quarter at dusk · sleep Hanoi
2. Hanoi · Train Street at dawn, Temple of Literature, Hỏa Lò, water puppets, bia hơi · Hanoi
3. Ninh Bình · Tràng An boat caves, Hang Múa sunset · Ninh Bình
4. Lan Hạ Bay · board the overnight cruise, kayak, sunset deck · on the boat
5. Bay sunrise → fly HAN→DAD → lantern-lit Hội An · Hội An
6. Hội An · An Bàng beach dawn, Ancient Town, basket boats, lanterns · Hội An
7. Hội An → Đà Nẵng via the Marble Mountains · Đà Nẵng
8. Hải Vân Pass by easy-rider, Lady Buddha, Dragon Bridge fire show · Đà Nẵng
9. Fly DAD→SGN · War Remnants Museum, colonial core, Bùi Viện street · Saigon
10. Củ Chi Tunnels, last bánh mì, evening flight home

**Why these dates:** Indian school-holiday fares fade by mid-June; Hội An/Đà Nẵng are in their sunniest season; we avoid Vietnam's 30 Apr–1 May crush. Hanoi will be hot (35 °C+) and Saigon gets a short afternoon shower — the plan uses early mornings and indoor/water time after lunch.

| | Budget | Mid-range |
|---|---:|---:|
| Return flights BLR↔DPS (IndiGo nonstop or AirAsia/Scoot 1-stop, June ×1.05) | ₹21,000 | ₹31,500 |
| 9 days on the ground (June stays ×1.05) | ₹26,955 (≈ ₹2,950/day) | ₹48,555 (≈ ₹5,300/day) |
| Extras: Visa on Arrival ₹2,600 · tourist levy ₹800 · SIM · insurance · contingency · shared driver/boat top-up | ₹9,950 | ₹12,600 |
| **Per head, all-in** | **₹57,905** | **₹92,655** |
| **Group of 3** | **₹1,73,715** | **₹2,77,965** |

**Start:** Sat 12 Jun — BLR check-in ~00:15, depart ~03:15 via KUL/SIN (or a nonstop if operating), arrive Denpasar ~15:30 local (est.).
**Return:** Sun 20 Jun — DPS night flight ~21:30, lands Bangalore early **Mon 21 Jun** (take Monday off). A morning return on 20 Jun lands the same evening.

**Day by day:** 1 Bangalore → Ubud · 2 Tegallalang rice terraces, Tirta Empul, Tegenungan · 3 Mount Batur sunrise trek, Monkey Forest · 4 Sanur fast boat → Nusa Penida (Kelingking, Broken Beach, Angel's Billabong, Crystal Bay) · 5 Manta snorkel or Diamond Beach → Uluwatu · 6 Padang Padang, Uluwatu Temple, Kecak at sunset · 7 Melasti → Canggu · 8 Surf lesson, Tanah Lot sunset · 9 Seminyak brunch → night flight home.

**Why these dates:** it is dry season (Mt Batur clear, Penida boats reliable), flights are cheaper than in May, and we leave before the Indonesian school holidays (~26 Jun) and the July price peak (+25–35 % on stays).

**Verdict:** Vietnam is ≈ **₹8,700 per head cheaper** (≈ ₹26,000 for three). Bali has the better weather in this window. If the budget stretches, Bali is the comfort pick; otherwise Vietnam is the clear value pick.

## 📅 Book in this order
- **Nov 2026:** set Google Flights alerts for CCU/BLR → HAN/SGN and BLR → DPS (±3 days); watch VietJet weekend sales and IndiGo/AirAsia sales.
- **Dec 2026 – Jan 2027:** buy the international flights (Vietnam as a multi-city: into Hanoi, out of Saigon); book cancellable first-night stays; buy travel insurance.
- **Feb – Mar 2027:** book the two Vietnam domestic flights (HAN→DAD, DAD→SGN) once dates are fixed.
- **Apr – May 2027:** reserve the Lan Hạ overnight cruise, Hội An stays, (Bali) Nusa Penida boat + stays; apply for the Vietnam e-visa 3–4 weeks before departure at https://evisa.gov.vn (US$25). Bali: Visa on Arrival at the airport (IDR 500k) + tourist levy online at https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id.
- **72 h before:** re-check baggage allowances, transit rules, weather for boats/cruise, and download offline maps.
